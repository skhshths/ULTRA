PERFORMANCE
ULTRASPEED
no swirls, no transitions, no pane animations, no scanlines.
100% speed, 0% looks.
warm tabs
hidden tabs kept loaded for instant switching.
more = snappier switching, more memory.
3
memory cap
past this, the tabs you used least long ago are put to sleep, one by one.
pages on screen and tabs playing sound are never touched.
pane stats
a slim header on every pane with its page's live memory, total memory & cpu
in the bar, plus ↻ to rebuild a bloated page. sampled only while on.
PRIVACY
block ads & trackers
ad networks and tracking beacons are refused at the network level.
sign-in providers are never blocked.
hide cookie banners
consent pop-ups from the common cookie tools are hidden and scrolling is given back.
the site sees no answer, so it treats you as having declined. "zap" in the palette hides anything else.
separate logins
workspaces lit here keep their own cookies & sign-ins: e.g. work accounts in 1, personal in 2.
needs macOS 14 or newer. their pages reload once when you switch this.
private workspace
cmd+opt+0. private tabs: no history, cookies, cache or session ever saved.
hidden from screen capture, locks after 5 min idle, 5 wrong passwords wipe it.
history
pages you visit are remembered on this mac to power address-bar suggestions.
never in the private workspace. cmd+d bookmarks a page.
